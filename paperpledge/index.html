Why this pledge?

AI is making scientific text easier and faster to produce. Careful research, original ideas, and readers’ attention remain scarce. We can use these tools to improve our work while concentrating on the contributions that matter.

The pledge

01 /
“I pledge to submit no more than one original research paper per calendar year arising primarily from my own research ideas. I remain free to collaborate on additional papers arising primarily from the ideas of others. I will prioritise careful work, meaningful questions, and contributions worth asking others to read.”

A personal commitment, freely made and based on trust.

A shared understanding

A personal commitment.

Room for collaboration, guided by good faith.

01One is a ceiling, not a target.
There is no expectation to submit a paper every year. Some research needs more time.
02Collaboration remains open.
The limit applies to papers arising primarily from your own research ideas. You remain free to collaborate on additional papers arising primarily from the ideas of others.
03Ideas often have shared origins.
Research ideas often emerge through collaboration, and their origins cannot always be neatly assigned. Each signatory interprets this commitment in good faith.
04Other contributions remain welcome.
Reviews, editorials, corrections, and teaching materials are outside the pledge.
05A commitment based on trust.
This is a voluntary, personal pledge, without formal accounting or monitoring.
